# Playground

Disposable 1280×720 prototypes. Use the design system; throw the story away or promote it.

1. Duplicate [Starter](?path=/story/playground-starter--default) into `Playground/<Name>.stories.tsx`.
2. Clone the template for the **level** you need (Home, FocusedRail, SingleInteractivity).
3. Fill slots with `@dtv/react`. Keep Overlay, Menu, and trail regions.
4. Read copy from `Pages/home` fixtures/mappers, not hardcoded button strings.
5. Move keepers to `Pages/` when they pass [Guidelines/Navigation](?path=/docs/guidelines-navigation--docs).

Do not add prototype screens to `@dtv/react`. Do not invent a second menu.
